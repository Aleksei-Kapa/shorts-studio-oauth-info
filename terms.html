Terms of use

These terms describe the intended use of Shorts Studio, a personal desktop video tool maintained by its owner. This website is informational and does not provide a public account or signup service.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Use an authorized channel

Connect only a YouTube channel you own or are authorized to operate. Google OAuth grants the application access to the selected channel. You remain responsible for the videos, images, voice, metadata, and publishing settings you authorize.

Manual and scheduled publication

The app can prepare videos for review or publish under the owner’s configured schedule. Enabling automatic publication authorizes uploads using those settings. Review the channel and visibility settings before enabling a schedule, and pause future runs when you no longer want automatic publishing.

YouTube may restrict uploads, thumbnails, or other features based on channel eligibility, API limits, account access, or its policies. Publication and thumbnail availability are not guaranteed.

Check generated content

AI-generated scripts, artwork, and narration can contain errors. Source checks and content review can reduce mistakes but do not guarantee accuracy. Review scientific claims and ensure you have the necessary rights to the content you publish. The app is intended for general educational videos and is not a source of professional advice.

Follow YouTube’s rules

Use the publishing features consistently with the YouTube Terms of Service and YouTube policies. Shorts Studio is an independent tool and is not endorsed by Google or YouTube.

Local operation and privacy

The application depends on the owner’s computer, installed models, and internet access for YouTube. Keep backups of projects you want to retain. Information about permissions, storage, revocation, and deletion is available in the privacy policy.
