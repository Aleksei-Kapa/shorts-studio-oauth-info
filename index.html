Created locally. Published through YouTube.
01 / Creation

Made on the Mac

Local AI models produce the text, artwork, and voice. Video projects and the topic history are stored on the owner’s computer.

02 / Publishing

An authorized channel

Google OAuth connects the chosen channel. YouTube permissions let the app verify that channel and upload videos with their metadata and covers.

03 / Control

Manual or scheduled

The owner can prepare a video manually or enable scheduled publishing. A local history helps avoid repeating topics and uploading duplicates.
