Privacy policy

Shorts Studio is a personal desktop application maintained by its owner. It creates videos locally and publishes to a YouTube channel only after its owner grants access.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Information the application uses

Local projects contain the chosen topic, source references, scripts, image prompts, generated images, voice recordings, captions, video files, covers, and publishing settings. The local history records used topics, generation states, and upload receipts to help prevent repetition and duplicates.

When you connect YouTube, the app receives OAuth access and refresh credentials from Google. It reads the selected channel’s identifier and name, and checks identifiers, channel ownership, and publication status for videos involved in its upload or recovery workflow. It does not request your Google password.

Why YouTube permissions are requested

youtube.readonly
Verify the connected channel and check relevant video metadata and status, including confirmation of an existing upload.
youtube.upload
Upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags, and publishing settings, and set video thumbnails when YouTube allows it.

These permissions support the channel connection and publishing features. They are not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated analysis.

Storage and protection

Projects and history are stored on the Mac where the application runs. OAuth credentials are kept in a separate local runtime folder with restrictive file permissions, outside video exports. Access credentials are refreshed through Google when needed.

The application has no developer-operated cloud storage or hosted account database. Its local text, image, and voice models generate content on the Mac. If the owner uses a separate automation assistant to operate the app or review content, that service’s own privacy settings and policies also apply.

When information is sent elsewhere

Google receives authorization and token-refresh requests. YouTube receives the videos, metadata, settings, and cover images submitted for publication. A video set to public, with its public metadata, becomes available to viewers through YouTube.

Shorts Studio does not sell Google user data, share it with advertising networks or data brokers, or use it to train AI models. The app uses and transfers information received from Google APIs only for the authorized channel and publishing features, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Local projects, history, and upload receipts remain until the owner deletes them. Credentials remain until the owner disconnects the channel or removes the local credential files.

You can revoke the app’s access from your Google Account connections. To remove information from this application, disconnect YouTube in Shorts Studio and delete its local project, history, and runtime files. Deleting local files or revoking access does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube; manage those videos in YouTube Studio.

For help with local deletion or privacy questions, contact aleks1kapa@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, access tokens, or private credentials by email.

This information website

This static website has no sign-in form, analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking code added by Shorts Studio. It does not connect your Google Account or receive the application’s projects or credentials.

It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.
